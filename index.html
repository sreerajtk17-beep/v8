Practice Spanish with AI 🇪🇸

Want to improve your Spanish through real, everyday conversations instead of boring vocabulary lists? Copy the prompt below and paste it into ChatGPT. Then enter your name, Spanish level, and a situation (for example: Alex, A1, café). ChatGPT will act as two Spanish-speaking friends, Joe and Helen, and you'll practice speaking with them naturally. They'll joke, react to what you say, introduce useful vocabulary, and gently correct your mistakes.

How to use it

  1. Copy the prompt below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT.
  3. Enter your Name, Level, and Situation.
  4. Start chatting in Spanish.
  5. Don't worry about making mistakes — just keep the conversation going!

Example: Alex | A1 | Café ☕
